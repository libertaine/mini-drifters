How to drift

          
    
            
  1. Steer into a corner and pull the handbrake until the tail steps out.
    2. 
            
  2. Stay on the throttle and steer the other way to hold the slide.
    3. 
            
  3. Lift off to straighten up.
    4. 
          

          
Scoring

          
Angle and speed on the road build unbanked points. Link drifts within two seconds to climb to ×3. When you straighten up, the points bank. Spin out, hit the wall or stay on the grass, and you lose them.
