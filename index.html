How to play

      

        

          
Driving

          

            
↑Accelerate RT

            
↓Brake, then reverse LT

            
← →Steer Left stick

          

        

        

          
Scoring

          
    
            
  • Angle and speed on the road build unbanked points. 25° to 50° pays best.
    • 
            
  • Link drifts within two seconds to climb from ×1 to ×3.
    • 
            
  • Straighten up and the points bank. Banked points are safe.
    • 
            
  • Spin out, hit the wall, stop making progress or stay on the grass, and you lose the unbanked points. The grass isn't part of the racing line.
    • 
          

        

        

          
Drifting

          
    
            
  1. Space / A is the handbrake. Steer into a corner and pull it for about a quarter of a second. Hold it too long and the car spins.
    2. 
            
  2. When the tail steps out, stay on the throttle and countersteer to hold the slide.
    3. 
            
  3. To recover, lift off the throttle and the car straightens up.
    4. 
          

          
With Power handling, the throttle can also break the rear loose, and the pedal sets the angle.
