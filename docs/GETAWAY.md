# Get the Footage

Open **Getaway → Get the Footage** and choose **Downtown** or **Industrial**. The mission uses the Classic or Power handling you selected. The game remembers that choice across categories and reloads.

## Film and escape

- Hit all four camera checkpoints in order, driving through each marked band in the direction it faces. The arrow and turn call show where to go. If you miss a camera, follow the recovery cue back to it.
- After camera four, cross the escape gate in the indicated direction. You have 120 seconds.
- The single pursuing cruiser fills **Bust Risk** when it stays close and can see you. Pull away or use a building to break its line of sight. A full meter ends the run.

## Showmanship and medals

Showmanship is banked drift plus destruction bonus. A fruit stand adds 150 points and a wooden barricade adds 250, once per object. Breaking them preserves a live drift combo and costs speed. Solid impacts still penalize the combo as usual. You must escape with every shot for your Showmanship to qualify.

| Environment | Handling | Bronze | Silver | Gold |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| Downtown | Classic | Escape | 600 | 1,600 |
| Downtown | Power | Escape | 900 | 2,600 |
| Industrial | Classic | Escape | 500 | 1,300 |
| Industrial | Power | Escape | 700 | 2,300 |

Getaway's provisional medals and bests last until you reload or close the page. Circuit records, Gymkhana records and saved reference ghosts use their existing separate storage.

**R** or controller **X** retries. **P** or the controller's **Menu** button pauses. **Escape** or **B** returns from an active Getaway run to its environment selection. From results, **Environments** reopens that same selection. **Return to title** is available on pause and results.
