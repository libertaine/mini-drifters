# Get the Footage

Open **Getaway → Get the Footage** and choose **Downtown** or **Industrial**. The mission uses the Classic or Power handling you selected. The game remembers that choice across categories and reloads.

## Film and escape

- Hit all four camera checkpoints in order, driving through each marked band in the direction it faces. The arrow and turn call show where to go. If you miss a camera, follow the recovery cue back to it.
- After camera four, cross the escape gate in the indicated direction. You have 120 seconds.
- The single pursuing cruiser fills **Bust Risk** when it stays close and can see you. Pull away or use a building to break its line of sight. A full meter ends the run.

## Showmanship and medals

Showmanship is banked drift plus destruction bonus. A fruit stand adds 150 points and a wooden barricade adds 250, once per object. Breaking them preserves a live drift combo and costs speed. Solid impacts still penalize the combo as usual. You must escape with every shot for your Showmanship to qualify.

| Environment | Handling | Bronze | Silver | Gold |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| Downtown | Classic | Escape | 600 | 1,600 |
| Downtown | Power | Escape | 900 | 2,600 |
| Industrial | Classic | Escape | 500 | 1,300 |
| Industrial | Power | Escape | 700 | 2,300 |

## Personal bests

Each mission, environment and handling pair keeps its own best Showmanship in this browser, so Downtown and Industrial in Classic and Power are four separate records. A best survives reloads, closing the browser and later visits in the same browser profile. Clearing the site's browser data removes it, and it doesn't sync between browsers or devices. There is no reset button, as for the circuit and Gymkhana records.

- Only a qualifying run sets a best: all four cameras filmed and an escape through the gate. A bust, running out of time, a cancelled upload, a retry or leaving the run never does.
- The highest Showmanship is kept. A lower or equal escape shows its own score and medal but leaves the best, and nothing is saved. A zero-point escape is a real record and earns Bronze.
- The medal is worked out from the saved best with the targets above. It is never stored separately, so it always matches the score.
- The environment sheet shows the best and its medal for the selected handling (`--` until you escape), and each environment button carries its medal. Results mark a saved improvement with `NEW BEST`, and `NEW MEDAL` when the medal is better than the one the old best held.
- If browser storage is unavailable, refused or full, the mission plays normally. The results show the run's medal as "not saved", and a note explains that improvements can't be saved. Damaged saved data is ignored and replaced by the next qualifying escape. Data saved by a newer version of the game is kept untouched and isn't shown or overwritten.

Getaway writes only its own record key. It never writes circuit records, Gymkhana records, laps or reference ghosts. The storage format is in [the records notes](RECORDS.md#getaway-records).

**R** or controller **X** retries. **P** or the controller's **Menu** button pauses. **Escape** or **B** returns from an active Getaway run to its environment selection. From results, **Environments** reopens that same selection. **Return to title** is available on pause and results.
